Why Young Eagles Matters

Young Eagles is EAA’s flagship youth initiative, introducing youth ages 8–17 to aviation through safe, memorable flights with experienced volunteer pilots.

At EAA Chapter 22, this program gives families across Rockford and Northern Illinois a first-hand view of flight and a clear path into aviation education.

What to Expect

Preflight Briefing

Each participant receives a concise safety and orientation briefing, including aircraft basics and what to expect before takeoff.

15–20 Minute Flight

Youth fly with a qualified volunteer pilot for an introductory flight that includes takeoff, in-flight learning, and landing.

Post-Flight Pathway

Families receive guidance on the next steps to keep learning, from student membership resources to local aviation opportunities.

After the Flight

For many youth, a Young Eagles flight is the first step in a longer journey.

  • EAA Student Membership and aviation learning resources
  • A practical flight-training pathway with local support
  • Scholarship and mentorship opportunities when available

Registration

Registration is completed through EAA’s official national Young Eagles system.
