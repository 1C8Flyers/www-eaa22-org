Young Eagles is EAA’s flagship youth aviation program — introducing young people to the world of flight through safe, memorable, introductory flights.

At **EAA Chapter 22**, we host Young Eagles events at **Cottonwood Airport in Rockford, Illinois**, giving local youth a positive first aviation experience in a welcoming community environment.

---

## Who It’s For

Young Eagles flights are open to:

- Youth ages **8–17**
- Families exploring aviation together
- Students interested in learning more about flight

No prior experience is required — just curiosity and excitement about aviation.

---

## What to Expect

Each Young Eagles event includes:

- A brief preflight orientation covering aircraft basics and safety  
- A flight with a qualified volunteer pilot (typically 15–20 minutes, weather permitting)  
- The opportunity to see takeoff, landing, and local scenery from the air  
- Time to ask questions and connect with pilots and volunteers  

Flights are provided by experienced volunteer pilots who share their time and passion for aviation.

---

## After the Flight

For many participants, a Young Eagles flight is just the beginning.

Youth are encouraged to continue exploring aviation through:

- EAA student membership opportunities  
- Online aviation learning resources  
- Local mentorship and educational pathways  
- Scholarship and flight training programs when available  

We are happy to help families understand next steps if interest continues after the flight.
